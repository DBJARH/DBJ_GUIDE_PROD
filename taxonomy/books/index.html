Version 0.1
Table of Contents

Each book is an item; and it is classified by the DBJ Taxonomy; each with an Item Declaration implemented as mark down document front matter.

The library items are book, classified and Item Declarations attached to each.

  1. Lisp in Small Pieces
---
# Item Declaration
title: Lisp in Small Pieces
taxonomy: "Conceptual: Application, Technology"
lifecycle: Published
tags: [lisp, scheme, interpreters, compilers]
owner: DBJ
author: Christian Queinnec
publisher: Cambridge University Press
year: 2003
isbn: "9780521545662"
