The Claim#

I am claiming that having BPT as organisation operating model makes “enterprise inteligence” praticularly natural to present itself as overarching model fulfilling its role. “Perfect match” use case, would be QPRO Business Inteligence.

The claim holds in part, and the weak point is the product layer.#

Where it holds

BPT places the failure QPRO addresses in the right spot. The “Defer platform migration” card shows a business decision (marked on track) contradicted by four technology facts: vendor support ending, hardware out of warranty, an audit, and a release dependency. That is the business-to-technology decoupling failing. The decision was declared without the technology context it depends on. QPRO’s “shows what isn’t” is that missing context made visible.

A rough mapping of its five dimensions onto BPT:

QPROBPT
Decision, AuthorityBusiness
ContextProduct, the declaration linking a decision to what it depends on
ExecutionTechnology
TimeCross-cutting

An overarching model needs one shared vocabulary across segments. BPT supplies that structure, so QPRO has something to be overarching over. Without it, “enterprise intelligence” is a dashboard over separate silos.

Where it is weak

  1. The unit differs. BPT’s linking unit is the product, while QPRO’s is the decision. The screenshot never shows a product. The cards are tagged by function (“HR · AI Tech”, “AI Tech”), not by B, P or T. Unless decisions are anchored to products, the mapping is your interpretation, not QPRO’s model.
  2. Static versus dynamic. BPT describes landscape structure, and QPRO describes state over time (windows, REM scores, “closes in 31 days”). BPT fits as the schema, but it doesn’t explain the temporal part.
  3. Consolidation comes first. By your own premise, the landscape must be consolidated before anything else. “Maps and connects in real time” depends on a consolidated landscape. On a fragmented one, you get the same inconsistency faster. The claim only works if BPT is the precondition, not a retrofit.

Test for the claim: can every QPRO card be expressed as a relation across business, product and technology elements? From the screenshot, the “Assign AI oversight owner” card (authority vacant, no technology link) fails it. I haven’t checked the qpro.ai site itself beyond this screenshot.
